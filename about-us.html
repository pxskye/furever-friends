HOW DID FUREVER FRIENDS CAME TO BE?

Recognizing the growing number of homeless animals in our community, a group of concerned citizens came together in 2025 to form FurEver Friends. Driven by a shared belief in the importance of responsible pet ownership, they established a shelter and began their mission to find loving forever homes for abandoned and neglected animals.
