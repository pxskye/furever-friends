In Case of Animal Cruelty Incidents

The Philippines' Animal Welfare Act of 1998 safeguards animals from cruelty and abuse. If you witness any act of animal cruelty, document it by taking photos or videos as evidence. Immediately report the incident to the nearest police precinct and request assistance from an officer to intervene at the scene.

We urge everyone to take an active role in protecting animal rights. If you need guidance, feel free to reach out to us—we're here to help.
